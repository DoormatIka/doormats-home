My Hero Academia

One of the first shonen anime I have ever watched.


I've watched this when I was a kid, but now I'm studying computer science. Even through these turbulent times, watching this as a wandering adult, I am still enamored by the scenes in this anime.


Talking briefly about the good parts of this show, the fight scenes are good. This is probably the most fundamental aspect of a shonen anime, they needed to nail this. And they did!

Also, this doesn't happen often. I felt my tears welling up in the emotional scenes. It caught me by surprise that they sympathized with the villains... but that's what cool heros do. Well done.


To be honest, I would not mind if MHA got #1 instead of Cinderella Gray on the monthly rankings.


Also, Toru Hagakure is so cute!
