Cinderella Gray


Cinderella Gray is the best sports anime I have ever seen!


I haven't been interested in sport anime so I haven't watched many of them. The only sports anime I was truly invested in was Inazuma Eleven, insane anime. Why are soccer balls heat-seeking missiles in there?


Anyways, I got an interest in Uma when Horse Race Tests got popular on Twitter. It was a series of crudely drawn horses bouncing in a stage, seeing what horse can reach the finish line.

It was a blast. I even made my cousin watch it and we had fun betting on what horse would win! There was a ton of fanart coming out of it as well. While browsing Twitter, looking at fanart, I found out that there was a horse racing game that's similar: Uma Musume.


I watched the first two seasons of Uma Musume: Pretty Derby and didn't finish the third season. The second season set the bar high in my opinion. The third season lacked.

I waited for a bit... then Uma Musume: Cinderella Gray was actually airing while Horse Race Tests was going on! Of course, I watched it and had a blast with the first 4 episodes.

I almost never watch currently running anime. I prefer it to build up so I can avoid the annoying cliffhangers anime sometimes has. Yet, I was so engrossed in the story and the running that I felt the need to follow it very closely.


It's peak, please watch it if you have the time.


Spy x Family


Spy x Family is a really comfortable anime for me.


I used to adore slice-of-life stories back in my childhood. The easily digestible format of it charmed me and the silly things the characters do. I still like some aspects of slice-of-life but I need a strong plot to follow in order to engage with shows nowadays.

Spy x Family feels like a combination of these two. A high-stakes plot yet still touching on slice-of-life at times.


Nothing else to say about it, it's a wonderful anime.

My Hero Academia

One of the first shonen anime I have ever watched.


I've watched this when I was a kid, but now I'm studying computer science. Even through these turbulent times, watching this as a wandering adult, I am still enamored by the scenes in this anime.


Talking briefly about the good parts of this show, the fight scenes are good. This is probably the most fundamental aspect of a shonen anime, they needed to nail this. And they did!

Also, this doesn't happen often. I felt my tears welling up in the emotional scenes. It caught me by surprise that they sympathized with the villains... but that's what cool heros do. Well done.


To be honest, I would not mind if MHA got #1 instead of Cinderella Gray on the monthly rankings.


Also, Toru Hagakure is so cute!

Cosmic Princess Kaguya

The best movie in 2026. I struggle to think of another anime that tops this.


This is a recent anime movie. It's notable for having every single big anime company and skilled animators on board with this anime.

So, what is this movie about? It's a retelling of Japanese folktale "The Tale of the Bamboo Cutter" but with gaming, fandom, and vtubing culture mixed into it. While most shows that try to do this seem cringy or try too hard to be quirky, this anime actually gets what internet culture is. Of course, you do not need to understand internet culture to watch this. It's optional, but watching this as someone who is well-versed in it is a delight.


Anyways, an overview of this movie is that Iroha, our main character, meets the princess of the Moon, Kaguya. They are complete opposites. Iroha is an independent girl who pays all of her bills, lives by herself, tries her hardest to study for Tokyo University. Kaguya however, is a free, impulsive, irresponsible girl that drains Iroha's bank account.

I won't spoil more of the story, as I highly highly recommend watching this movie for yourself. It made me smile, laugh, walk around in my room because I was too excited... It made me tear up more than watching other anime, matching my reaction to A Silent Voice.

I'm not saying that this movie has the story of A Silent Voice. Cosmic Princess Kaguya's story is simple... but it left an impact on me personally. You may not like it as much as me, all I'm saying is that there is a strong potential that it will leave an impact on you.


For a movie of this caliber, the animation is good. Unbelievably good. It's as if God wanted to gift us an anime to make up for his previous creation (One Punch Man Season 2-3). The mix of CGI and traditional animation has left me stunned, since I did not know this mix is possible, let alone look this good. In all my years of watching anime from my childhood, I never wanted so badly to live in this movie, to experience the sceneries and breathtaking beauty of the world. It's a beautiful anime.

It's a truly wonderful movie. This is my #1 of this year, and I do not know how anything will top it. It's as if I could feel each spark of inspiration and talent from this. It's a labor of love. Also, my twitter is full of CPK retweets...
