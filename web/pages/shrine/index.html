This is dedicated to Touhou Project!


I've been a looong time fan of Touhou ever since 2020, also known to me as Satan's year.

So many terrible things happened in that year, the start of the global pandemic, the acceleration of the division between people, etc. As that was occuring, I started diving deeper in osu! tournaments and Touhou content.

But before telling this story, I'd like to explain what Touhou actually is.


Touhou Project is a very popular Japanese indie franchise centered on challenging bullet hell shoot 'em up games. All the mainline games are created by one person, ZUN (Jun'ya Ota) of Team Shanghai Alice. It also has many official spin-offs, official manga, and music albums.



Dai Karasu's LoLK Stage 6 in 4K 16:9

Touhou has a massive set of distinct female characters based on Japanese mythology. It's mostly known for being a massive goldmine of quality music and fanworks, due to ZUN's lenient rules for Touhou's copyright. Most Japanese artists got their start on this franchise, or was inspired by someone who took inspiration from it.


Continuing on from before, I dove deeper in Touhou content in the midst of the pandemic. I discovered a metric ton of good music from this series, so much that 90% of my YouTube playlist are Touhou remixes! Most of the art that pixiv and twitter recommends me are mostly Touhou related, it's amazing how much dedication these people have.

This passion is also a source of inspiration. I wish to create art or games as fun or advanced as what some of these fans do.

Touhou Mystia Izakaya

A comfortable cooking game that punches well above its price range. The gameplay loop is simple: grab ingredients at daytime, serve dishes to the costumers at nighttime. You can also finish quests from other characters to get new dishes! I'd like to create something close to this.



Yoiyami Dancers

It's a rhythm game that incorporates the bullet-hell aspect of Touhou with rhythm-based movement. It's difficult to grasp at the start but you get your groove moving as you go along, similar to dancing! It's also cheap for what quality it gives. Five-ish dollars for a polished rhythm game, a must buy in my opinion.
